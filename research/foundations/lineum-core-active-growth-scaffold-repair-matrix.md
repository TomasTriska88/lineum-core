## 1. Owner hypothesis

At the verified negative-result gate, the project owner proposed:

> Yeast makes dough from flour and water, while a mould gives it shape.

This is an owner-generated candidate hypothesis, not evidence.

Operational mapping for the first pilot:

- flour and water: matched weak `psi` and `phi` material;
- yeast `Y`: slow `mu` accumulation acting through the active `1 + mu` feedback multiplier;
- mould `S`: a structured static `kappa` activity/permeability map;
- dough: the developed live `psi`–`phi` state;
- cutting the dough: erasing one angular sector of the developed ring;
- removing the mould: replacing structured `kappa` with a mean-matched uniform map.

The analogy is not universal. `mu` is not called DNA, semantic memory, life, or biological yeast, and `kappa` is not assumed to be a literal physical mould.

## 4. Retrieved implementation facts

Active source: `lineum_core/math.py`, blob SHA `bb877021810691223a0eb960a45493a2e351112a`.

- `kappa` is a supplied spatial array and is returned unchanged by the NumPy step.
- `kappa` multiplies stochastic generation, noise, `phi` interaction, `phi`-gradient drift, `psi` diffusion, `phi` accumulation/diffusion, and `mu` growth.
- `mu` is always read through `1 + mu`; `use_mu` controls updating, not reading an already nonzero `mu`.
- With `use_mu=True`, `mu` accumulates above a relative `psi`-energy threshold and decays slowly.
- The active solver has no target image, missing-region detector, or direct restoration term.
- Returning amplitude may therefore be passive diffusion, continued forcing, confinement, or global amplification rather than repair.

These are software facts only.

## 9. Frozen geometry and initial material

Grid and coordinates:

- `32 × 32`;
- `x,y = linspace(-1,1,32)`;
- radius `r = sqrt(x²+y²)`;
- angle `theta = atan2(y,x)`.

Target ring profile:

```text
ring = exp(-((r - 0.45) / 0.10)^2)
```

Initial arrays:

```text
psi = 0.30 * ring * exp(i * theta)
phi = 0.02 * ring
mu = 0
```

Structured scaffold:

```text
kappa_structured = 0.55 + contrast * (ring - mean(ring))
```

The construction preserves exact mean `kappa=0.55`. The corresponding no-scaffold control is `kappa_uniform = 0.55` everywhere. Any cell violating `0 < kappa <= 1` is invalid; the frozen contrasts below are expected to remain valid and must be checked before execution.

## 11. Frozen damage intervention

Damage region:

```text
ring > 0.20
and
abs(theta) <= pi/4
```

This removes the right-hand 90-degree sector of the developed ring.

At the damage time:

- set `psi=0` inside the mask;
- set `phi=0` inside the mask;
- preserve `mu` inside and outside the mask;
- preserve `kappa`;
- do not copy any value from the no-damage twin after intervention.

Preserving `mu` is intentional: the first test asks whether a slow internal trace can guide return of the fast state. A later discriminator may erase `mu` in the damaged region if the first matrix is positive.

## 12. Frozen stages

1. **Formation:** advance each lane and its twin for the cell’s formation horizon.
2. **Damage:** apply the frozen mask only to the damaged run.
3. **Repair:** advance damaged and no-damage twins for 40 matched deterministic steps.
4. **Scaffold removal:** in structured-`kappa` lanes, replace `kappa` with uniform `0.55` while preserving `psi`, `phi`, and `mu`; advance for 40 steps. Uniform lanes continue unchanged for matched comparison.

No second damage after removal is included in this feasibility matrix.

## 13. Frozen observables

For each cell and lane record:

- hashes of final `psi`, `phi`, and `mu`;
- exact equality against the no-damage twin;
- global and damage-region `psi` energy;
- immediate post-damage deficit relative to the twin;
- final deficit after repair;
- recovery fraction:

```text
(E_damaged_after - E_damaged_immediate)
/
(E_reference_after - E_damaged_immediate)
```

- damage-region amplitude correlation against the twin when both variances are nonzero;
- outside-mask normalized RMS error;
- total `psi`, `phi`, and `mu` ledgers;
- maximum `abs(psi)`;
- non-finite, clipping, saturation, and fail-safe indicators;
- retention after scaffold removal relative to the pre-removal repaired state and matched twin.

Recovery fractions may be below zero or above one and must not be clipped.

## 14. Preregistered feasibility verdict

A cell provisionally supports the combined implementation only if all conditions hold:

1. `Y1S1` recovery fraction exceeds `Y1S0`, `Y0S1`, and `YS00` by at least `0.10` absolute;
2. `Y1S1` outside-mask NRMSE is no more than `0.10` worse than the best single-factor lane;
3. `Y1S1` final total `psi` energy is no more than `25%` above its no-damage twin;
4. `max(abs(psi)) < 10`;
5. no non-finite values, fail-safe reset, or invalid `kappa` occur.

Interpretation rules:

- similar recovery in all lanes means passive common dynamics;
- benefit only from `Y1S0` means the structured scaffold is unnecessary in that cell;
- benefit only from `Y0S1` without local energy return means passive shaping/confinement;
- broad global amplification without selective deficit reduction is not repair;
- benefit lost after scaffold removal means distributed environment-dependent maintenance;
- persistence after removal is not autonomous repair until a second post-removal damage test succeeds;
- failure of all eight cells rejects only this active `mu × kappa` grid under these conditions.

The `0.10` and `25%` cutoffs are feasibility thresholds, not universal physical constants.
