Eq-11.1 is an old engine blueprint whose missing pages are still being recovered. Blueprint archaeology is useful, but it should not delay watching the real boat that is already in the water.

## 4. Four evidence layers

### 4.1 What the current implementation computes

At the inherited implementation cutoff:

- the complete `F0` source contains a coherent phase-aligned conditional mean and centered stochastic innovations;
- at exact vacuum and `kappa = 1`, the coherent component pumps amplitude at `0.015` per unit model time;
- the centered innovation variance rate scales with numerical step size and vanishes under refinement;
- `phi` and `mu` can carry state and feedback, but neither closes the source-energy account;
- `kappa` is supplied rather than canonically evolved;
- current `mu` reinforces existing routes through `1 + mu` but does not encode missing geometry;
- current static `kappa` can shape transport but does not itself provide active repair, stored deformation, or reciprocal return.

### 4.2 What was reproducibly observed

The retained observations include:

- pump only increased smooth field norm but produced no winding defects in `12/12` P1 seeds;
- centered randomness nucleated dense winding activity in `12/12` seeds;
- adding the coherent pump suppressed the tested defect population while increasing norm;
- centered-only formation through model time `20` left a source-off late multi-defect signal in `12/12` P2 seeds through the tested horizon `60`;
- over model time `40-60`, that remnant had mean defect count `20.5232`, final-frame mean `15.6667`, mean resolved-core fraction `0.974687`, minimum resolved-core fraction `0.941535`, mean overlap `0.99999892`, and mean orientation `0.990655`;
- the remnant did not pass the whole-movie population rule and did not establish persistent object identities;
- complete `F0` formation-only histories reached winding extinction in `12/12` seeds at mean model time `28.13` while retaining larger smooth norm;
- exact state, configuration, and RNG transfer reproduced short continuation bit for bit, while resetting future RNG changed `psi` by up to `0.06480561760422429`;
- a static recipe under independent development did not reconstruct the donor exactly, and retaining the recipe beside live state had no runtime effect;
- the tested existing-field passive membrane candidates failed the full reversible-storage gate;
- current deterministic and stochastic parent-to-descendant copying produced zero candidate descendant frames across `352` trajectories;
- the first `mu x kappa` repair matrix had no supportive combined cells; the maximum absolute `mu` recovery effect was `0.00004216379362752265` and structured `kappa` acted only as a weak passive shaper.

### 4.3 Cautious interpretation permitted by those observations

The centered-only source-off remnant is the most mature current-Core candidate for a long-lived source-independent population process. It is not yet a localized particle, identity, lineage, repaired object, or physical excitation.

The existing repair and copying results imply that exact replay, broad morphological resemblance, passive shaping, activity amplification, and content-specific reconstruction must remain separate claims.
