## 1. Answer first

These are not three names for one mechanism.

- **Eq-11.1 is a local growth-and-leakage equation.** A local `Psi` pattern grows or decays according to `Phi`, scalar leakage, and a phase-gradient penalty. The documented law has no independently audited finite fuel stock.
- **The epsilon cycle adds a new environmental stock.** A separate `epsilon(x,y)` field is intended to limit intake and receive declared leakage back from the active structure. It is a reservoir/circulation architecture, not merely a retuned Eq-11.1 coefficient.
- **Relic Foam is a residual field state.** It is described as a multi-node mesh left after a prepared structure collapses. It is not itself a new field or a complete equation. Its proposed function is to shape later transport and release transient fragments when struck.

Plain picture:

- Eq-11.1 is an engine whose throttle and exhaust depend on local conditions.
- The epsilon cycle adds a fuel tank and return pipe to that engine.
- Relic Foam is the deformed, still-warm material left on the ground after an earlier engine or vehicle breaks apart.

A working engine, a fuel circuit, and wreckage that affects later traffic can interact, but they are not interchangeable explanations.

None of the three families is presently authorized for a new numerical repair experiment. Eq-11.1 and epsilon lack a recovered complete executable receipt. Relic Foam has a documented sequence of positive and negative claims, but its cited material and re-ignition runners are not currently recovered, and the later documented tests qualify the strongest early claims: the foam ages, true re-ignition fails, and repeated emission exhausts the material.

The primary numerical programme remains unchanged: recover and replay the retained P2 package, then classify the observed centered-only source-off remnant over a longer horizon. The present three-family work is a parallel, non-blocking provenance lane.

## 11. Why the families must not be merged

### 11.1 Shared metaphors do not imply shared mechanisms

All three families use language such as growth, leakage, environment, fuel, healing, or stability. Those words conceal different causal graphs.

- Eq-11.1 changes the local derivative.
- Epsilon adds a transferable state.
- Foam conditions later evolution through a history-dependent residual configuration.

A shared `metabolism` or `environment` metaphor is not a shared equation.

### 11.2 Combining them would double-count support

A foam state allegedly produced by an Eq-11 variant cannot independently validate Eq-11. A prepared epsilon-supported structure cannot validate spontaneous Eq-11 formation. A foam that helps a later object survive cannot prove that the foam generated that object.

Each dependency must be explicit:

```text
parent mechanism -> parent structure -> collapse -> foam -> later environmental effect
```

Evidence at the final arrow does not validate every earlier arrow.

### 11.3 Different failures require different repairs

- Eq-11.1 failure: local survival versus decay/runaway and missing provenance.
- Epsilon failure: incomplete stock closure and terminal evaporation.
- Foam failure: parent dependence, aging, transient emission, and fatigue.

Retuning one family cannot silently rescue another.

## 12. Comparison with established physical systems


The existence of dissipative solitons in optics shows that gain-loss stabilized localization is physically possible in some systems. It does not validate the Lineum equation, identify a Lineum structure as a particle, or close its energy ledger.

```text
metastable network + local rearrangement + history-dependent mechanical response
```

Important mismatch:

```text
physical foam consists of bubbles/interfaces with measured material laws;
Relic Foam is a historical numerical Psi/Phi pattern with no recovered executable or dimensional mapping
```

Shape resemblance or local gap closure is therefore only a hypothesis generator.
