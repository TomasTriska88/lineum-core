## 2. Historical separation rule

The following corpora must remain distinct until a specific historical relationship is supported:

```text
A. individual Nag Hammadi and related early Christian texts;
B. Greek, Jewish, philosophical, medical, and Christian uses of pneuma;
C. the composite Jewish apocalyptic collection called 1 Enoch;
D. related Enochic works such as the Book of Giants;
E. the diverse Jewish manuscripts grouped as the Dead Sea Scrolls.
```

The Dead Sea Scrolls and 1 Enoch are not to be labeled Gnostic merely because later writings share motifs of light, hidden knowledge, ascent, spirit, cosmic rulers, or layered worlds.

The category `Gnosticism` itself is contested in modern scholarship. This audit therefore works text by text and motif by motif, not by assuming one unified ancient system.

## 3. Evidence layers

Every retained claim must be labeled as exactly one of:

1. **Textual witness:** what a named text or manuscript actually states.
2. **Historical interpretation:** what critical scholarship cautiously infers.
3. **Structural abstraction:** a domain-neutral systems pattern extracted from the text.
4. **Lineum hypothesis:** a mechanism, observer, intervention, ledger, or toy model inspired by that pattern.
5. **Physical correspondence:** a claim about nature, allowed only after independent empirical evidence.

This report may contain layers 1 through 4. It contains no layer-5 validation.
