## 3. Meaning of "the same things as in the universe"

Visual resemblance is insufficient. A Lineum phenomenon counts as a candidate connection only when the comparison is operationally defined across the following dimensions.

1. **Object or response class** — what measurable structure, relaxation, memory, or scaling behaviour is being compared.
2. **Causal input** — what source or perturbation is supplied and what is deliberately withheld.
3. **Observable** — the exact quantity extracted from Lineum and the corresponding real-world measurement.
4. **Scaling law** — whether the response changes with size, source strength, time, or environment in the observed way.
5. **Robustness** — whether the result survives timestep, resolution, seed, boundary, observer, and mechanism-off controls.
6. **Parameter economy** — whether one frozen parameter set explains multiple systems or whether each target is separately retuned.
7. **Cross-observable consistency** — whether the same mechanism predicts more than one independent measurement.
8. **Physical bridge** — whether units, dimensions, symmetries, conservation properties, and causal structure can be mapped without contradiction.

A result that matches only one shape after tuning remains phenomenology. A result that survives interventions and predicts multiple independent observables with a frozen mapping may advance toward an empirical connection.

## 4. The three-question universe map

### Q1 — Emergent galactic radial response

Lineum question:

Can a visible-matter-like source, supplied without a dark-halo profile or an inserted `1/r` law, generate a robust long-range response whose circular-motion proxy becomes approximately flat or otherwise follows measured galaxy scaling relations?

Candidate real-universe anchor classes to retrieve and define:

- resolved galaxy rotation curves;
- the radial acceleration relation;
- baryonic Tully-Fisher-type scaling;
- diversity of inner rotation-curve shapes;
- weak and strong gravitational-lensing constraints where relevant;
- disk stability and non-circular motions;
- cross-galaxy universality versus galaxy-specific retuning.

Minimum success is not one fitted galaxy. The mechanism must first pass synthetic known-answer controls, then a preregistered multi-galaxy split with a frozen observer and tightly controlled parameter freedom.

### Q2 — Emergent bounded saturation and genuine attraction

Lineum question:

Do current or historically relevant Lineum equation families possess naturally bounded attracting regimes, or do apparent endpoints arise from clipping, explicit saturation, dissipation, finite observation windows, boundary recurrence, or a non-identifying observer?

Candidate real-universe anchor classes to retrieve and define:

- stable and metastable localized field structures;
- nonlinear saturation and pattern selection in known field systems;
- cosmological late-time attractor language and its actual observational constraints;
- scalar-field fixed points, vacuum stability, and phase transitions;
- black-hole-like or compact-object attractor claims only when a public mechanism supplies an operational bridge;
- return after perturbation, basin structure, and timescale separation.

A bounded state is not automatically a universal attractor. A dissipative localized structure is not automatically a cosmological endpoint. The experiment must identify the precise mathematical type of attraction and the domain of its basin.

### Q3 — Emergent information retention during relaxation to a scalar minimum

Lineum question:

When distinguishable initial states relax toward the same scalar minimum, is their distinguishing information destroyed, hidden in unresolved degrees of freedom, transferred to `phi`, retained in `mu`, encoded in phase or topology, or merely slow to decay?

Candidate real-universe anchor classes to retrieve and define:

- unitary versus dissipative evolution;
- decoherence and environmentally distributed records;
- entropy growth and coarse-graining;
- symmetry breaking and vacuum relaxation;
- topological or phase memory;
- black-hole information questions only through publicly defined, operationally relevant analogues;
- experimentally known memory effects in nonlinear and open field systems.

Equal final energy or equal local amplitude does not prove information loss. Information retention must be measured by the distinguishability of paired states under declared observers, including observers deliberately denied access to selected channels.

## 5. Evidence ladder

Every candidate connection receives one of the following maximum statuses.

0. `source_recovered` — a public or repository source contains the stated claim or mechanism.
1. `implemented` — a current or historical Lineum code path contains the exact operation.
2. `reproduced` — a frozen run produces the declared internal observable.
3. `robust_within_tested_domain` — appropriate nulls, interventions, and numerical checks support the observation.
4. `mechanistically_supported` — discriminating interventions favour the proposed causal mechanism over registered alternatives.
5. `dimensionless_empirical_correspondence` — a preregistered Lineum observable matches a real dataset or scaling relation without an arbitrary per-target remapping.
6. `physically_bridged` — dimensions, units, symmetries, conservation properties, and multiple independent observables share a consistent frozen mapping.
7. `canonical_bounded_claim` — the exact limited statement passes Lineum code and whitepaper promotion gates.

No source, simulation image, low error, green test, or analogy may skip a level.

- videos, presentations, talks, interviews, and demonstrations;

Prefer primary and authoritative sources:

- peer-reviewed papers and preprints with reproducible methods;
- official mission, survey, observatory, or collaboration data releases;
- established public benchmark datasets;
- review articles for orientation, followed by the relevant primary source;
- official documentation for numerical or observational standards.

For contested topics, record competing interpretations and identify which measurements are widely agreed even when their explanation is disputed.

## 8. Emergence anti-cheat rules

A candidate result is rejected or downgraded when any of the following occurs:

- the desired radial shape, fixed point, or memory trace is inserted directly into the source, boundary, observer, or loss function;
- parameters are selected after seeing the held-out target without a new preregistered test;
- a local cap or clip is described as natural saturation;
- periodic-image recurrence is described as long-range interaction;
- slow decay within a short run is described as permanent memory;
- an observer is chosen because it displays the desired shape while reasonable alternatives fail;
- each galaxy, object, or initial state receives its own unconstrained coupling;
- a dimensionless resemblance is presented as a physical prediction in SI units;
- an external public claim is treated as verified merely because its code or presentation is public.

## 9. Gate before the next simulation

No new consequential simulation lane may be selected until its active report records:

- the exact real-universe observable;
- the public source and dataset provenance;
- the exact Lineum variant and implemented equation;
- the proposed observer and dimensional status;
- at least two credible alternative explanations;
- null controls and mechanism-off interventions;
- success, failure, and inconclusive thresholds;
- parameter freedom and held-out data policy;
- the interpretation allowed by each possible outcome.
